**Document Version:** 1.0.0  
**Governing Document:** Maa Mitahara Website PRD v2 (Oct 6, 2026), Requirements MS-2 & SF-3  
**Regulatory Framework:** FSSAI (Advertising & Claims) Regulations, ASCI Guidelines, DPDP Act 2023  
**Status:** Living register. Every marketing badge, trust line, and health claim must be logged here. Any claim without signed legal/clinical approval is blocked or replaced.

---

## 1. Governance Principles

1. **No Therapeutic / Disease Cure Claims:** Food products must never claim to cure, prevent, or diagnose medical conditions (e.g., "cures gestational nausea", "treats anaemia", "guarantees breast milk supply").
2. **Explicit Qualification:** Broad claims like "Doctor Recommended" or "Doctor Inspired" are strictly retired in favor of specific attributions: `"Reviewed by Dr. [Name], [Qualification]"`.
3. **Traceable Proof:** Every claim must point to an internal lab certificate, physical label declaration, or documented clinical review.

## 3. Universal Disclaimers

The following mandatory disclaimers must appear visibly across the site:

### General Medical Disclaimer (`SF-4`)
> *"Nutrition support, not medical advice. Traditional food preparations are intended to complement, not replace, clinical antenatal care. Always consult your obstetrician or healthcare provider regarding specific dietary requirements and medical conditions."*

### Quiz & Plan Disclaimer (`SF-4`)
> *"This nutrition plan is curated based on stage and reported dietary preferences. It does not constitute a clinical diagnosis or medical prescription. If you have gestational diabetes, pre-eclampsia, allergies, or are on prescribed medications, review this plan with your doctor before consumption."*
